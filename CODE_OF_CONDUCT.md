# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a small project built around a chronic illness;
plenty of the people here are managing diabetes themselves or caring for someone
who is. Assume good faith, keep criticism about the code, and remember there's a
person on the other end.

## Not okay

Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
publishing someone's private information, unwelcome sexual attention, and
sustained disruption. Also: dismissiveness or "advice" about how someone manages
their own condition. This isn't the place for it.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer via a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/nvmddev/sugarglider/security/advisories/new)
(it's the private channel this repo has) or by emailing the address on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that don't follow this
document, and may block repeat offenders.
